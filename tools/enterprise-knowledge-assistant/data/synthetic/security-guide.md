# Account Security Guide

## Password reset
Use the company identity portal to reset your own password. The service desk will never ask you to send your password or one-time code by email or chat.

## Lost device
Report a lost company device to the service desk immediately. Do not attempt to remotely wipe a device unless the security team instructs you to do so.

## Suspicious messages
Forward suspicious email to the security team using the report-phishing button. Do not open unexpected attachments or follow sign-in links in the message.
