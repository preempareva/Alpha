# Employee Travel Policy

## Claim limits
Employees may claim up to INR 2,500 per night for domestic hotel accommodation when the trip is approved in advance. Claims require an itemised receipt and must be submitted within 30 days after the trip.

## Eligibility
This policy applies to full-time employees travelling for approved business. Contractors must follow the terms in their individual agreement.

## Approval
The employee's manager approves the trip before booking. Finance reviews submitted claims. This document is fictional training data and is not an actual company policy.
