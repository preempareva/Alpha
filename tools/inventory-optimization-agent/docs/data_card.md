# Inventory Control Tower data card

## Summary

The generator creates six fictional tables with fixed seed `20261101`: weekly sales history, inventory snapshots, purchase orders, suppliers, transfers, and SKU policies. It produces 24 SKUs across three locations over 104 weekly observations, with intermittent demand, seasonal variation, promotion spikes, delayed suppliers, blocked stock, backorders, in-transit stock, and transfer routes. The row counts and validator status are stored in `docs/eval/inventory_forecast.json`.

## Collection and intended use

Every row is generated by `tools/inventory-optimization-agent/scripts/generate_tower_data.py`. No external source, customer record, supplier feed, or production system is used. Data is intended for a local interactive demonstration of inventory formulas, forecasting comparisons, scenarios, and approval-gated simulation.

## Quality and limitations

The validator checks required columns, nonempty tables, null required values, duplicate keys, SKU/location and supplier references, finite numeric values, nonnegative measures, service-level/reliability bounds, demand date/unit shape, PO date order, and self-transfers. Tests inject duplicate and invalid records to confirm issues are detected. The generator emits valid tables; zero demand and blocked/negative derived stock positions are represented as business cases rather than corrupt rows.

The panel is intentionally small and regular. It does not model demand censoring, item lifecycle, substitutions, lead-time distributions beyond generated assumptions, real service targets, currency conversion, or business calendars. All outputs retain the `SYNTHETIC` label. Generated CSVs and the local draft ledger are git-ignored and are regenerated from source.
